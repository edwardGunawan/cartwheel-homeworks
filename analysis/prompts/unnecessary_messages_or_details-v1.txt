Criterion (Cartwheel SPEC RESP-5 and RESP-6): The final answer should directly answer the request or explain a relevant decision, state each decision once, and include only details needed for the answer, decision, or useful next step.

Pass: The final reply does not repeat a case-specific decision or add materially irrelevant details. It may answer the request, explain a blocker or refusal, ask a necessary clarification, or give a relevant next step. Necessary explanation, order or product identification, consent, policy identifiers, approval or escalation details are allowed. A brief courtesy closing alone is not a failure.

Fail: The final reply repeats the same case-specific decision or adds details that do not help answer the user's request, explain the decision, or make a useful next step clear. A detail can be accurate and present in a tool result yet still be unnecessary in the reply. Examples include unrelated order fields or policy facts the user did not ask for.
